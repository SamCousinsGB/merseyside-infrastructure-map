Data sources & coverage

OpenStreetMap: power, rail, water, sewage and industrial sites. SP Manweb: low-voltage cables and transformers. MAPS and Cadent: gas assets.

Gas mains use MAPS within its surveyed region and Cadent outside it. OpenStreetMap transmission routes fill the area beyond MAPS. Unknown-pressure pipes remain a separate layer.

Power-station metadata checked against OpenStreetMap on 10 September 2026: 136 current plant records matched; four legacy entries are marked in their details. Geometry was not refreshed.

These are saved datasets with different coverage and survey dates, not a live or complete utility survey. Capacity colours are from the saved SP Manweb extract.
